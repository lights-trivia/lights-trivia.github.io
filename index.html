📜 House Rules

the fine print, but fun
👥 Teams
  • About 5 people per team.
  • Each team picks a captain. Only the captain's answer counts.
  • Talk it over, but quickly: the clock is ticking!
🙋 How to answer
  • Shout the color, hold up 1 to 4 fingers, or type the number in the meeting chat.
  • Answer before the timer hits zero. Late answers don't count.
  • True or False questions have just two tiles.
🏆 Scoring
  • Correct answer: 1000 points.
  • Wrong or no answer: 0, no penalty.
  • The final question is worth double (2000).
  • Answer 3 in a row right and your team goes 🔥 on fire.
  • Tied teams share the same place. Tie for 1st? A sudden-death question decides it (+500 to the winner).
